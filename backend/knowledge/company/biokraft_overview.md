# BioKraft Foods Private Limited — Company Overview & Operations Manual

## 1. Company Profile & Operational Mission
BioKraft Foods Private Limited is a specialized B2B and D2C food processing and plant-based protein manufacturing company in India. We operate high-capacity food processing plants with strict quality and SLA guarantees.

## 2. Core Operational Constraints
- **Perishable Inventory**: Finished products and raw plant proteins have strict shelf-life constraints requiring tight supply-demand matching.
- **Capacity Limits**: Operational throughput is constrained by internal processing line capacity (typically 3,500 kg/month baseline) and co-manufacturing partner capacity (800 kg/month expansion ceiling).
- **Maintenance & Reserves**: Internal plants require scheduled downtime for sanitization and maintenance (150 kg/month loss), plus safety reserve buffering (200 kg/month) to shield contractual obligations from unexpected equipment failures.

## 3. Customer Segments & SLA Commitments
- **B2B Institutional Buyers**: Supermarkets, hotel chains, and food service partners (e.g., Metro Hypermarket, Reliance Retail, Taj Hotels).
  - SLA Penalty: Non-delivery incurs high financial penalties (10x shortfall weight penalty in LP solver) and contractual breach risk. Target SLA fulfillment is 95%+ (98% under Max Revenue policy).
- **D2C Consumer Retail**: E-commerce, quick-commerce (Blinkit, Zepto, Swiggy Instamart), and direct webstore.
  - Flexibility: High demand elasticity; lower shortfall penalty weight compared to B2B, serving as a demand buffer during capacity deficits.
